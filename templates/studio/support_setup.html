How fan support works

  • Available to active Free, Standard and Premium creators.
  • Creators must connect a valid Paystack payout subaccount.
  • Supporters can choose a suggested or custom contribution amount.
  • Supporters may provide a name, email and message, or choose to be displayed anonymously.
  • Kalxa applies the commission recorded when the payment is initialized.
  • A browser redirect alone never confirms a payment.
  • Only independently verified payment-provider information can mark a contribution as paid.
  • Contributions do not automatically grant access to subscriber-only content.
