How activation works

Every creator can start on the Free plan. If you choose Standard or Premium during registration, your paid features will only become available after your once-off Yoco payment has been securely verified.

Standard costs R300 once-off. Premium costs R500 once-off. There are no monthly Kalxa platform subscription fees for these plans.

Already on Standard? You can upgrade to Premium for R200 once-off, provided your previous Standard activation payment has been verified.
